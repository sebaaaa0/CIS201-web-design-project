About Whiskers & Beans

Founded in 2026 in Riyadh, Whiskers & Beans Cat Cafe brings together specialty coffee and rescued cats in one warm, relaxing space. Our goal is to give guests a calm place to study or unwind while helping local rescue cats feel safe and loved.

"A cup of coffee is even better when a sleepy cat curls up beside your chair." - Whiskers & Beans Team

How a Visit Works

  1. Check in at the front counter and order your favorite drink.
  2. Sanitize your hands and remove your shoes at the Cafe entrance.
  3. Enjoy your time with Pumpkin, Oreo, Luna, and Mocha!

What Makes Us Special

Rescue Partnership
All four of our resident cats were rescued locally and receive regular vet care.
Separate Cafe & Seating Zones
Our espresso bar is kept separate from the cat play area for hygiene and comfort.
Study friendly Corners
Quiet tables with soft lighting just watch out for Oreo near your pens!

Cafe Fun Fact

Over 120 cups of iced Spanish latte are served every weekend while Oreo knocks at least 5 napkins onto the floor!
